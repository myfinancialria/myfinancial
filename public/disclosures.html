Section 03 · Investor charter

Investor charter.

Published pursuant to SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/80 dated June 02, 2025 (Investor Charter for Investment Advisers), read with the Master Circular for Investment Advisers dated June 27, 2025. A copy of this charter is provided to every client at on-boarding.

A

Vision & Mission

Vision. Invest with knowledge & safety.

Mission. Every investor should be able to invest in the right investment products based on their needs, manage and monitor them to meet their goals, access reports and enjoy financial wellness.

B

Details of business transacted by the Investment Adviser with respect to the investors

  1. To enter into an agreement with the client providing all details including fee details, aspects of conflict of interest disclosure and maintaining confidentiality of information.
  2. To do a proper and unbiased risk-profiling and suitability assessment of the client.
  3. To obtain registration with the Know Your Client Registration Agency (KRA) and the Central Know Your Customer Registry (CKYC).
  4. To conduct audit annually.
  5. To disclose the terms and conditions of advisory services offered to the client, including fees, related documents, risk factors and refund policy.
  6. To maintain records of interactions with all clients, including prospective clients (prior to on-boarding), where any conversation related to advice has taken place.
C

Details of services provided to investors

  1. On-boarding of clients: sharing of the agreement copy; completing KYC of clients.
  2. Disclosures to clients: full disclosure about the adviser's business, affiliations and compensation in the agreement; no access to the client's accounts or holdings for offering advice; disclosure of the client's risk profile back to the client.
  3. Investment advice: advice based on the client's risk profiling and suitability, taking into account available investment alternatives.
D

Grievance redressal mechanism and timelines

  1. Level 1 — the Investment Adviser. Complain directly to the adviser in writing (see Grievance Redressal below). Complaints are resolved within 21 calendar days of receipt.
  2. Level 2 — SEBI SCORES 2.0. If unresolved or the resolution is unsatisfactory, lodge the complaint on SCORES 2.0 at https://scores.sebi.gov.in. SCORES provides a two-level review: first review by the designated body (BASL), second review by SEBI.
  3. Level 3 — Online Dispute Resolution. If still unsatisfied, initiate dispute resolution through the SMART ODR portal at https://smartodr.in — online conciliation and, thereafter, online arbitration.
E

Rights of investors

  1. Right to privacy and confidentiality.
  2. Right to transparent practices.
  3. Right to fair and equitable treatment.
  4. Right to adequate information.
  5. Right to initial and continuing disclosure, including of any conflict of interest as and when it arises.
  6. Right to fair and true advertisements.
  7. Right to awareness about service parameters and turnaround times.
  8. Right to be heard, and to satisfactory and timely grievance redressal.
  9. Right to exit from a financial product or service in accordance with the terms agreed.
F

Do's and Don'ts for investors

Do's

  1. Always deal with SEBI-registered Investment Advisers; check for a valid registration certificate and SEBI registration number. The list of all registered IAs is available on the SEBI website.
  2. Pay only advisory fees to your Investment Adviser, through banking channels only — including CeFCoM — and maintain duly signed receipts mentioning the details of your payments.
  3. Always insist on risk profiling before accepting advice, and on advice strictly based on your risk profile, taking into account the available investment alternatives.
  4. Ask all relevant questions and clear your doubts before acting on advice; assess the risk–return, liquidity and safety aspects before investing.
  5. Insist on getting the terms and conditions in writing, duly signed and stamped, and read them carefully — particularly those relating to advisory fees, plans and the category of recommendations — before dealing with any adviser.
  6. Approach the appropriate authorities for redressal of doubts and grievances, and inform SEBI about any Investment Adviser offering assured or guaranteed returns.

Don'ts

  1. Don't fall for stock tips offered under the pretext of investment advice.
  2. Do not provide funds for investment to the Investment Adviser.
  3. Don't fall for the promise of indicative, exorbitant or assured returns. Don't let greed overcome rational investment decisions.
  4. Don't fall prey to luring advertisements or market rumours, or act only on the basis of phone calls or messages.
  5. Do not be swayed by limited-period discounts, gifts or other incentives, and don't rush into investments that do not match your risk appetite and goals.
  6. Do not share the login credentials or passwords of your trading, demat or bank accounts with anyone, including the Investment Adviser.
G

Responsibilities of investors

  1. Verify the adviser's SEBI registration before availing any service.
  2. Provide accurate, complete and current information for KYC and risk profiling, and update the adviser on material changes in your financial situation.
  3. Read all documents — particularly the agreement and the Most Important Terms & Conditions (MITC) — before signing.
  4. Pay fees only through traceable banking channels and preserve the records of payment.
  5. Monitor your investments regularly and seek clarification wherever required.
  6. Raise grievances promptly through the mechanism prescribed, escalating to SCORES 2.0 and ODR where needed.

Section 05 · Grievance redressal

How to raise a grievance.

Step 1

Write to the adviser

E-mail info@myfinancial.in with the subject line "Grievance – Investment Advisory". Every complaint is acknowledged, and resolved within 21 calendar days of receipt.

Step 2

Escalate to SEBI — SCORES 2.0

If the response is delayed or unsatisfactory, lodge a complaint on SEBI's centralised complaint redress system at https://scores.sebi.gov.in. SEBI's toll-free helplines: 1800 266 7575 / 1800 22 7575.

Step 3

Online Dispute Resolution

If the outcome on SCORES is still unsatisfactory, initiate online conciliation and arbitration through the securities-market ODR portal at https://smartodr.in.

Section 06 · Investor safeguards

Safeguards you can hold us to.

  1. Fees are payable only for advisory services. No funds or securities are ever accepted into the adviser's account — implementation happens in your own bank, broking and demat accounts.
  2. We will never ask for your login credentials or OTPs for trading, demat or bank accounts. Treat any such request, from anyone, as fraud.
  3. Advisory fees can be paid through CeFCoM, SEBI's centralised fee collection mechanism, in addition to regular banking channels.
  4. No assured, guaranteed or risk-free returns are ever promised. Report anyone promising them to SEBI.

Section 08 · Conflict of interest

Conflict of interest.

myfinancial operates a fee-only, zero-commission model. The practice earns no distribution income, brokerage, referral fees or commissions from any product manufacturer, distributor, broker or platform — its only revenue is the advisory fee paid directly by clients. Advice is therefore not influenced by any third-party compensation. Should an actual conflict of interest nonetheless arise in any specific circumstance, it will be disclosed to the affected client in writing before advice is rendered, so that the client can act with full knowledge of it.
