What the practice does

Advice, in writing.

Three services, one engagement. Every recommendation follows a documented risk profile and suitability assessment, and reaches you in writing.

01

Financial planning

Cash-flow and net-worth mapping, emergency-fund sizing, insurance adequacy review, tax-aware structuring and goal identification — a written plan that fits your life, not a product list.

Plan first, products last
02

Investment advice

Risk profiling and suitability assessment first, then asset allocation and selection of investments appropriate to your profile — including zero-commission direct plans where suitable.

Suitability before selection
03

Reviews & rebalancing

Periodic portfolio reviews against the plan, rebalancing advice when allocations drift, and plan updates when your income, goals or residency change.

Discipline, on a calendar

Execution always stays in your hands. Advice is implemented by you, in your own bank, broking and demat accounts. The adviser never handles client funds or securities, and never asks for account credentials or OTPs.

How an engagement runs

A process you can audit.

Step 01

Terms before advice

You receive the Most Important Terms & Conditions (MITC) and the client agreement — fees, scope, rights and exit terms — before anything is signed. KYC is completed through KRA / CKYC as prescribed.

Step 02

Risk profiling

A documented assessment of your capacity and tolerance for risk, your horizons and constraints. No advice is rendered until your profile is recorded and shared with you.

Step 03

The written plan

Financial plan and asset allocation, matched to your risk profile and goals, with the reasoning stated. Suitability is the test every recommendation must pass.

Step 04

You implement

Advice is executed by you in your own accounts, at your pace. Implementation guidance is part of the service; handling your money is not, and never will be.

Step 05

Reviews & records

Scheduled reviews, rebalancing advice and updates as regulations or life change. Records of advice and interactions are maintained as the regulations require.

Fees & conflicts

One fee. No other income.

The practice is fee-only: clients pay a transparent advisory fee, charged strictly within the modes and limits prescribed under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013. Fees are payable through banking channels — including CeFCoM, SEBI's centralised fee collection mechanism — and are set out in the agreement before any advice is rendered.

There is no distribution income, brokerage, referral fee or commission from any product manufacturer, distributor, broker or platform. Because no one else pays, the advice answers to no one else. Any actual conflict of interest that arises will be disclosed to the client in writing before advice is rendered.

No assured, guaranteed or risk-free returns are ever promised. Anyone promising them is breaking the law.
